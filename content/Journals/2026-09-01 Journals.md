### <center>does the transition from August to September feel more like a beginning or an ending? Has it always felt this way?</center>

<center>Fall is my one true love. She comes and finds me sunkissed on the beach and moves my hair with a gentle breeze. She brings blankets of orange, red, and yellow leaves to warm me as the sun hides away in the clouds. Her silhouette creates a shade upon the log I rest my head upon.</center>
<br>

<center> When I was younger and fall came around I often saw it as a chance of renewal. Most of my summers were spent up the coast with my family, leaving a distance between me and my friends at home.</center>
