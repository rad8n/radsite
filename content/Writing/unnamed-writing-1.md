<center> Reminiscing on my childhood often brings me back to one particular location, or rather area. I grew up in a small suburb nestled between the ocean bay and a large green space behind our house. To a kid with such fervour for exploration and imagination it provided a realm of escape. </center>

<br>
<center> To describe the physical arrangements of locations in relation to one and other may fail to communicate the grandeur these grounds provided. Instead I will attempt to describe each location individually, and let you fill in the blanks as I did</center>


## <center> The Garden</center>
<br>

<center> Within the bramble of the forest, a small clearing lay. With ever spreading blackberries lining the edges, and branches covered in scattered snowberries looming over the path further into the forest. Green grass grew throughout the clearing shared with the neighbours. Below a large tree resided a small wooden garden bed, slightly elevated from the grass and brimming with crop. A few steps away you would find yourself at the base of a wooden ladder leading into our treehouse on stilts. While it was always full of spiders, I made every attempt I could to spend time in there. A small , old rowboat lies in the middle of the clearing. At first glance it may appear forgotten, covered in growth and slowly being consumed by the Earth. Upon closer inspection, however, you see love that was poured into it. Flowers line the bottom edges, surrounding the trunk of a small tree sprouted out of the center. </center>
